PureFrame is a clean, local-first video player. Hardware decoding with
FFmpeg software fallback (H.264/HEVC/VP9, DTS/AC3/EAC3 audio), BT
streaming (libtorrent4j), DLNA + Google Cast to TVs, auto-loading

Media stays on your device.

=== Notes (per App Inclusion Policy) ===

- APK is ~92 MB — above the 30 MB rule of thumb. Reason: bundled FFmpeg
  software decoding with the full codec set (H.264/HEVC/VP9 +
  DTS/AC3/EAC3) plus libtorrent4j. Size is inherent to soft-decode media
  players; an exception as granted for comparable players would be
  appreciated.
- Permissions: READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (+ legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE,
  maxSdk 32) to scan local videos (core feature). No
  MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (removed in v1.1.1). INTERNET for
  user-initiated BT/HTTP downloads and DLNA/Cast on the home network.
  FOREGROUND_SERVICE (+ MEDIA_PLAYBACK / DATA_SYNC) / POST_NOTIFICATIONS
  for playback notification and download tasks.
- usesCleartextTraffic="true": required for DLNA/UPnP and the local HTTP
  streaming proxy (media player home-network use case).
- No trackers, no analytics, no ads, no self-updater. Zero data
  collection.
