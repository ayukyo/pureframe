**Description**
PureFrame is a clean, local-first video player. Hardware decoding with FFmpeg software fallback (H.264/HEVC/VP9, DTS/AC3/EAC3 audio), BT streaming (libtorrent4j), DLNA + Google Cast to TVs, auto-loading external subtitles, and a natural-ordered same-folder playback queue.

Works fully offline. No trackers, no analytics, no ads, no accounts required. Media stays on your device.

### Further Notices
- APK is ~92 MB — well above the 30 MB rule of thumb. Reason: bundled FFmpeg software decoding with the full codec set (H.264/HEVC/VP9 + DTS/AC3/EAC3) plus libtorrent4j. We understand the size, it's inherent to soft-decode media players, and hope for an exception as granted for comparable players (e.g. OptiCast #659, mpv-based). Happy to discuss.
- Permission usage: READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (+ legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, maxSdk 32) for scanning local videos (core feature). No MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — removed in v1.1.1. INTERNET for user-initiated BT/HTTP downloads and DLNA/Cast on the home network. FOREGROUND_SERVICE (+ MEDIA_PLAYBACK / DATA_SYNC) / POST_NOTIFICATIONS for playback notification and download tasks.
- usesCleartextTraffic="true" is required for DLNA/UPnP and the local HTTP streaming proxy — media player home-network use case, acceptable per your policy.
- No trackers, no analytics, no ads, no self-updater. Privacy policy: https://ayukyo.github.io/pureframe/privacy-policy.html
- Fastlane metadata (en-US + zh-CN, 3 phone screenshots) is in the repo.
