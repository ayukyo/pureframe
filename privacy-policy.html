Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

PureFrame (纯帧) does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

PureFrame（纯帧）不收集、不传输、不分享任何个人数据。

English

  • PureFrame works entirely offline and on-device. Your videos, downloads, and preferences never leave your phone.
  • No analytics. No tracking. No ads. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No account required. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • Permissions are used only for core functionality:
    • Videos / Media permissions — to scan and list the videos already on your device.
    • Storage — only to save torrents/downloads to the folder you choose.
    • Network — to download files you explicitly add (magnet / torrent / direct links) and to stream local files to DLNA/Cast devices on your own Wi-Fi.
    • Notifications — download progress and playback controls.
    • Display over other apps — only if you enable the floating mini-player.
  • Crash logs, if you choose to report one, are sent manually by you (e.g. via GitHub Issues) and contain only what you include.

中文

  • PureFrame 完全离线、本地运行。你的视频、下载、偏好设置不会离开手机。
  • 无统计、无追踪、无广告。应用不包含任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。
  • 无需账号，没有任何登录注册环节。
  • 权限仅用于核心功能：
    • 视频/媒体权限 —— 扫描并列出你设备上已有的视频。
    • 存储权限 —— 仅用于把磁力/种子/直链下载保存到你选择的目录。
    • 网络 —— 下载你主动添加的任务，以及在你自己的 Wi-Fi 内把本地文件串流到 DLNA/Cast 设备。
    • 通知 —— 下载进度与播放控制。
    • 悬浮窗 —— 仅当你启用悬浮小窗播放时使用。
  • 崩溃报告仅在你主动提交时（如 GitHub Issues）才会离开设备，内容以你提供的信息为准。
