Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

🎬

Local video playback

Automatic library scanning with hardware / software / auto decoding — software decoding adds DTS/AC3/EAC3 audio and H.264/HEVC/VP9. Speed 0.5x ~ 3x.

📱

Vertical swipe playback

Short-video-style interaction: swipe up for the next video, down for the previous one, with auto-advance — or switch back to classic brightness / volume gestures.

📺

DLNA / Google Cast

Discovers TVs and set-top boxes on your network automatically; push playback with volume and seek control. Degrades gracefully without GMS.

🧲

Magnet / torrent / direct-link downloads

Magnet links, .torrent files, and HTTP direct downloads. Parallel tasks, background service, Wi-Fi-only option.

⚡

Stream while downloading

Start watching at ~10% downloaded, seek freely within the cached range, and downloading is never slowed by playback.

💬

Subtitles & queue playback

Automatically loads matching SRT/ASS/SSA/VTT subtitles; episodes in the same folder play in sequence with natural sorting.

🔒

Privacy first

No data leaves your device, no analytics SDKs, no ad frameworks. Fine-grained media permissions only — no "all files access". Read the privacy policy →
